# Articles (a, an, the)

**a / an** are indefinite articles. They point to any one member of a category, not a specific item the reader can already identify.
- Choose between *a* and *an* by the sound that follows, not the letter. Use *a* before a consonant sound and *an* before a vowel sound.
- Examples: *a university*, *an hour*, *an SUV*
- Indefinite articles attach only to singular countable nouns. A plural noun takes no article, or takes *some*, but never *a* or *an*.

**the** is the definite article. It marks a noun the reader can pin down: something named earlier, something unique, or something narrowed by context.

## Rules

**Skip the article** with non-countable nouns used in their general sense (advice, sand, courage, traffic).
- *Patience is required for fieldwork.* / *Traffic is worse on Fridays.*

**Add "the"** when a modifying phrase or clause narrows that same non-countable noun to one particular instance.
- *The patience she showed during the delay surprised everyone.*

## Do NOT use "the" before:
- Names of countries (Japan, Peru) — except island-nation names (the Philippines), state titles with a descriptive noun (the United Arab Emirates), or names fixed by convention (the Congo)
- Names of cities, towns, provinces (Chicago, Newark)
- Names of streets (Baker Street)
- Names of individual lakes and bays (Lake Tahoe) — except lake groups (the Finger Lakes)
- Names of individual mountains (Mount Fuji) — except mountain ranges (the Rockies) or a handful of named peaks by tradition (the Eiger)
- Names of continents (South America)
- Names of single islands (Sicily) — except island chains (the Aleutian Islands)

## DO use "the" before:
- Rivers, oceans, seas (the Danube, the Pacific, the Adriatic Sea)
- Fixed points on the globe (the Prime Meridian, the South Pole)
- Named geographic or cultural regions (the Arctic, the Balkans)
- Deserts, forests, gulfs, straits, peninsulas (the Gobi, the Strait of Gibraltar)
