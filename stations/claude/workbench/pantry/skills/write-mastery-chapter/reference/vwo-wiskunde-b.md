# Dutch VWO wiskunde B, the baseline for "Prior knowledge"

Prior knowledge is everything the rest of the chapter's theory builds on. It has two sources: this list (wiskunde B) and the earlier chapters of the same course.
Only skills the chapter actually uses before teaching them are repeated. Everything else is either not needed or new material, and new material belongs in the
chapter's own Theory. This list is a baseline: check it against the reader's actual course when you can, and correct it here.

## Assumed known (Prior knowledge may repeat it, and only if the chapter uses it)
- **Algebra and equations.** Rewriting expressions, powers and roots, fractions, solving equations and inequalities, two equations with two unknowns
  (substitution, elimination), quadratic equations and the abc-formula.
- **Functions and graphs.** Domain and range, transformations of graphs, inverse functions, power functions, exponential functions and the number e,
  logarithms with their rules, the trigonometric functions sin, cos, tan (in radians), the graph of a parabola, the sign of a quadratic term.
- **Differentiation of one variable.** The derivative as slope of the tangent, difference quotient and limit, tangent line, sum, product, quotient and
  chain rule, the derivative of powers, e^x, ln x, a^x, sin, cos, the second derivative, stationary points, extrema, inflection points.
- **Integration.** Antiderivatives, the definite integral as area, substitution in simple cases.
- **Geometry with coordinates.** Lines, distance, circles, vectors in the plane and space, the dot product (inproduct), parametric equations.
- **Sequences and sums.** Arithmetic and geometric sequences and their sum formulas.

## Not part of wiskunde B (teach it in the chapter's Theory)
- Matrices and matrix products, the transpose, matrix inverse and determinant as tools (only the 2 by 2 case may be met in vector geometry).
- Sigma notation with indices used for general sums, index notation such as x_i and A_ij, the Kronecker delta.
- Partial derivatives, the gradient, the Jacobian, the Hessian, derivatives of vector functions, the chain rule with Jacobians.
- The hyperbolic tangent, the sigmoid and the ReLU.
- Quadratic forms, eigenvalues, definiteness, convexity, least squares.
- Probability distributions beyond the basics, estimation, likelihood (wiskunde A and statistics courses, not B).

## How to use it
1. List every skill the chapter's exercises need before they are taught in the chapter itself.
2. Mark each as "on this list" (Prior knowledge, from wiskunde B) or "from chapter N" (Prior knowledge, from an earlier chapter) or "new" (chapter Theory).
3. Write a Prior block only for skills that are needed, and keep the block short: the rule, one example, a few drill exercises.
